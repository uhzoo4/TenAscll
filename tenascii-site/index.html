THREE MECHANICS

Not a metaphor. The actual mechanism.

Every visual idea in this project maps to one real line of physics code. Nothing here is decorative.

01

TURNSTILE

A ring drawn around a central singularity. Any forward particle that crosses into it is auto-inverted on the spot — no keypress, no trigger, just geometry. It flips color and starts rewinding the exact path it just walked.

02

THE ALGORITHM

Squad A launches, waits until the turnstile has actually converted fifteen of its particles, then squad B launches — timed so the two squads are guaranteed to occupy the same space while moving in opposite entropy directions. A real pincer, not two waves that happen to overlap.

03

PARADOX

Forward matter meeting its own inverted matter isn't survivable. Both particles are deleted on contact, and the impact site smoulders — white-hot core, then orange, then a fading ember — for about 1.4 seconds. A paradox has a cost, visibly.

HOW IT WAS BUILT

Four phases, in the order they actually happened.

  1. 01

    The turnstile

    Replaced a manual invert-all keybind with a real spatial trigger: a ring around the singularity that auto-inverts anything crossing into it.

  2. 02

    The Algorithm

    Scripted the two-squad pincer so the second wave launches only once the turnstile has actually converted enough of the first — guaranteed convergence, not luck.

  3. 03

    Paradox consequences

    Forward/inverted collisions now annihilate both particles and burn down through white, orange, and ember instead of a single flash frame.

  4. 04

    Trails, logs, and a real file structure

    Split one growing file into five focused modules, added history-fade trails and a live event log, and made the whole thing portable to a second platform.
